Privacy note · 11 November 2026

Your game and your information

The White Whale versus Old Thunder is an educational game by Sam O’Brien-Olinger, Sam Tim Solutions, Ireland. Contact samobrienolinger@gmail.com about privacy, corrections or information requests.

Playing the game

The game does not ask you to register an account, enter a real name or submit contact information to play. The chase uses page state for your role, coordinates, attempts and result. This privacy update adds no analytics, advertising pixels, account system or score-upload service.

Hosting is still a data flow

GitHub Pages delivers the website. Loading a page or asset sends technical connection information, such as an IP address and requested resource, to the host for delivery and security. This is separate from optional advertising tracking. See GitHub's privacy statement for its processing, retention and international-transfer arrangements. The game cannot delete provider-held logs through a local reset button.

External links and email

Developer and source links lead to independent websites. Their own privacy rules apply when you visit them. Email you choose to send includes your address and message and is used to respond and manage the enquiry, based on the legitimate interest in communicating with people who contact the project. Send only what is necessary, not passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

Retention of correspondence depends on resolving the enquiry, maintaining a necessary record and any applicable legal requirement. Ask about a specific record rather than assuming a fixed automatic deletion date.

Your rights

Where the project processes personal information, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability where applicable, and object to legitimate-interest processing. Rights have legal conditions and exceptions. Contact the email above; the usual GDPR response period is one month, subject to permitted extensions and proportionate identity checks. You may complain to Ireland's Data Protection Commission.
